## Dependencies and Execution Order

### Phase Dependencies

- **Setup (Phase 1)**: T001 verifies owner-supplied immutable tests and tools. T002 follows
  only after those prerequisites are present.
- **Foundational (Phase 2)**: T003, T004, and T005 depend on T002 and can run in parallel.
  All user stories depend on these shared modules.
- **User stories**: US1 is the first independent increment. US2, US4, and US3 depend on US1.
  Execute them in this plan order because they extend shared controller/UI files; the final
  story order respects the specified P1-before-P2 priorities.
- **Polish (Phase 7)**: Depends on all four user stories. Complete T022 after the automated gates in T021 and before the final checkpoint.

### User Story Dependencies

- **US1 (P1)**: Depends on Setup and Foundational phases; no dependency on another story.
- **US2 (P1)**: Depends on US1's saved list and form.
- **US4 (P1)**: Depends on US1's save flow; adds startup restoration and recovery behavior.
- **US3 (P2)**: Depends on US1's list and totals; adds filter state and subset calculations.

### Parallel Opportunities

- T003, T004, and T005 touch separate modules and can proceed in parallel after T002.
- In US1, T006 (controller) and T007 (renderer) can proceed in parallel; T008 integrates
  them.
- In US2, T010 (controller) and T011 (UI) can proceed in parallel; T012 follows both.
- In US3, T015 (controller) and T016 (UI) can proceed in parallel; T017 follows both.
- The story phases are kept sequential because they extend shared application/UI files.

## Implementation Strategy

### MVP

US1 is the first product increment: add and review expenses, with validation and totals.
Include the US4 startup-restoration slice before calling the persistence promise release-ready,
because an add/list-only session would not satisfy FR-012.

### Incremental Delivery

1. Complete Setup and Foundational work.
2. Deliver US1, then verify using the immutable owner-supplied tests.
3. Add US2 edit/delete behavior and re-run its tests.
4. Add US4 reload/recovery behavior and re-run persistence tests.
5. Add US3 filters and filtered totals.
6. Complete cross-cutting accessibility, responsive, security, performance, and full-gate checks.
7. Perform and record the manual browser review in T022 before the final checkpoint.
